No Guessing Games

How payment works — Ohio & Illinois.

We believe you should know what you're paying, every time. Where you live determines whether you can use insurance — here's exactly how it works.

In Ohio? Use your insurance.

For Ohio patients, we'll bill insurance directly. We're in-network with Medicare, Medicaid, and most major plans including Aetna, Anthem (BC/BS), UnitedHealthcare, Humana, Cigna, Medical Mutual, Caresource, and Buckeye. For covered visits, you pay only your normal copay or deductible — not the self-pay price.

In Illinois, or prefer to self-pay?

All Illinois visits are simple self-pay — we don't bill insurance in Illinois. The same applies anywhere if you're uninsured, out-of-network, or just prefer to pay privately: our flat self-pay rates above are exactly what you'll pay. No claims, no surprise bills. We can also provide an itemized superbill you may submit to your own insurer for possible out-of-network reimbursement.

A note for Ohio Medicare & Medicaid patients: We accept Medicare and Medicaid in Ohio. Visits covered by your plan are billed to your plan — we won't charge you a self-pay rate for a covered service. Self-pay pricing applies to services your plan doesn't cover, or where paying privately is permitted. (In Illinois, all visits are self-pay.) When you reach out, we'll tell you exactly how your specific visit will be billed before you commit to anything.
