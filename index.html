How care is structured

From the first call to ongoing care

A one-off problem is just a visit. If you have a condition that needs following, this is the rhythm after that first appointment.

First contact

First visit

Services

What we treat

Everyday care

The things that just come up

Sinus and respiratory infections, sore throat, urinary tract infections, rashes, pink eye, stomach bugs, minor injuries. Prescriptions sent to your pharmacy, lab work ordered locally, and a note for work or school when you need one.

Routine

Annual visits, labs and refills

Yearly check-ins, screening labs, medication refills, birth control, immunization review, and referrals to specialists when something needs a closer look.

Primary care

Chronic disease management

Hypertension, diabetes, heart failure, COPD, chronic kidney disease, thyroid disease and hyperlipidemia. Includes medication management and refills, lab and imaging orders, and specialist referrals.

CCM

Chronic care management

Monthly care coordination for patients with two or more chronic conditions, including a written care plan. Covered by Medicare and most plans.

APCM

Advanced primary care management

Continuous primary care with 24/7 access to the care team and management of the full problem list.

RPM

Remote patient monitoring

Connected blood pressure cuffs, scales, glucometers and pulse oximeters. Readings are reviewed by the practice and used to adjust treatment.

Coordination

Care coordination

Direct communication with home health nurses, facility staff, caregivers and treating specialists.

How visits work

Video or phone, wherever you are.

Visits are conducted over secure video or by telephone. When a patient is with a home health nurse or in a senior living community, staff can be present to assist with the examination and report vital signs, wound status and other findings.

New patients complete an intake and records release before the first visit. Established patients are seen on a recurring schedule based on their conditions.

This is a telehealth practice. There is no clinical office — the Canfield address is used for mail and records only, and patients are not seen there.
